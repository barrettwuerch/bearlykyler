FOR WHOEVER HAS HIM NEXT

He wakes when the page does and picks something to do on his own. If you would rather choose, the controls under him will take a request — he finishes what he is doing first, then obliges.

He can be picked up. Take hold of him and he will hang there patiently; let go and he drops, rights himself, and carries on as though nothing happened.
